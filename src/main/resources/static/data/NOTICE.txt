Municipality catalog

The municipality names in municipalities.json are adapted from
spanish-cities-info 2.4.2 (https://github.com/ManuelCebreiro/spanish-cities-info),
which reports that its 8,132 municipalities are verified against official INE data.
Postal codes are based on the package's optional postal-code dataset, derived from
the INE electoral census street index (July 2026 edition) and municipality directory,
cross-referenced by INE municipality code. Some municipalities have multiple postal
codes, which the form presents for selection.
